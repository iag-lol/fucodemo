FAQ

Frequent questions

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote builder: pick services, deliverables and a date, and we reply with a proposal. You can also reach us by WhatsApp or email.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on scope. The timeline is set in the proposal; if you need same-day assets, tell us when requesting the quote.

Do you shoot photo and video on the same day?

Yes. We can put together a crew that covers both formats in a coordinated way.

Do you work outside Santiago?

Tell us where your project is and we will include it in the proposal along with travel.

How do I receive the files?

Through a private code-protected gallery where you can pick favourites and download your files.
