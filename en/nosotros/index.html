How we work

From idea to delivery

Five stages, with what we do and what you get in each one.

01/ 05

  1. 01 To be confirmed

    Brief and proposal

    We start by understanding what you need to say, to whom, and on which screens it will be seen.

    • A meeting to learn about the project and its goals
    • Visual references and tone
    • List of deliverables, formats and uses
    • Proposal with scope, fees and timeline

    What you getA clear proposal before you commit to a date.

  2. 02 To be confirmed

    Pre-production

    The shoot is won beforehand: we arrive knowing which moment cannot be missed and who covers it.

    • Location visit or review
    • Shooting schedule and shot list
    • Crew roles and equipment
    • Accreditations and permits

    What you getA shared schedule for the day.

  3. 03 To be confirmed

    Shoot day

    On location, photo and video work in step without interrupting what is happening.

    • Set-up and tests before start time
    • Coverage following the shot list
    • Quick social assets when agreed
    • Footage backed up at the end of the day

    What you getEvery key moment captured in the agreed formats.

  4. 04 To be confirmed

    Post-production

    Selection, edit, colour and sound with a single eye, so photo and video feel like one body of work.

    • Photo selection and retouching
    • Editing, music and sound design
    • Colour grading
    • Landscape and vertical versions

    What you getA first cut to review and comment on.

  5. 05 To be confirmed

    Delivery

    You receive everything organised, in every format you need to publish.

    • Changes based on your feedback
    • Exports for each format and platform
    • Private gallery with access code
    • Favourites and downloads

    What you getYour final files in a private gallery.
